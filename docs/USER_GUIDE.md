# AuraSOS user guide

For parents, caregivers and teachers. No technical knowledge needed.

> AuraSOS is a calming aid, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat anything. Keep using the
> strategies your child's care team recommends.

## What AuraSOS does
AuraSOS plays gentle, predictable sound and shows slow, soft visuals to help a child settle. It was
designed around how many autistic children experience sound and light: sudden sounds, high pitches and
flicker can hurt, while steady, slow, low sounds and predictable motion can calm. Everything the app
does is therefore **slow, soft and capped**.

It works **without internet**, has **no ads, accounts or tracking**, and keeps everything on your device.

## The home screen
Four large buttons, built to be pressed quickly in a hard moment:

| Button | What it starts |
|---|---|
| **SOS** (big heart) | The calming session for a meltdown. It shows which mode it starts underneath: *Calm down* or *Deep calm* |
| **Focus** | Quiet music with a slowly vanishing rainbow timer: good for homework or a transition |
| **Play & calm** | A touch-to-play canvas: every touch makes a soft ripple and a gentle note |
| **Deep calm** | Brown-noise "weighted blanket" sound with a low drone, no rhythm: for when music is too much |

A very faint **gear** sits in the top-right corner. That is the caregiver area (see below).

### Start faster: the long-press menu
Press and hold the app icon on your home screen (Android and iPhone) to jump straight to **SOS: calm down,
Focus, Play & calm** or **Deep calm**. On the web version, installed to the home screen on Android, the same menu
appears. The shortcut only starts from the home screen: it never interrupts a session in progress.

## What happens during a session
- The sound **fades in over 4 seconds**, so it never startles.
- A small, quiet label at the top names the mode (for *Calm down* it also shows the stage:
  **Matching → Easing → Settled**). It fades away after a few seconds so the picture stays clean.
- The screen is **locked** for the child. Tapping, swiping or pressing back will not exit. Touches make
  soft ripples and gentle notes instead. Notes always sound pleasant together; the child can't play a "wrong" one.
- The screen stays on (phone apps; on the web the device may still dim it).

### Calm down: the three stages
SOS follows a clinical idea called the *iso-principle*: meet the child where they are, then lead them down.

1. **Matching (first 3 minutes):** a steady warm rhythm at a pace close to an agitated heartbeat, so it
   doesn't feel dismissive.
2. **Easing (next 5 minutes):** the tempo slows, the rhythm melts into soft pads, the sound gets warmer and the
   picture slows down.
3. **Settled (from about minute 8):** a slow heartbeat-like pulse with soft brown noise, and the screen dims.

### Unlocking (for you)
Press and hold **both top corners of the screen at the same time for 3 seconds**. A faint ring fills in each
corner while you hold. Letting go of either corner starts the count again, so a child tapping at random cannot unlock it.

After unlocking you are asked, with one tap, **how it went**: 🙂 helped, 😐 no change, 😣 made it worse.
This builds your child's "what helps" history. Tap the small ✕ to go back to the session without rating.

## The caregiver area
**Hold the faint gear in the top-right corner of the home screen for 3 seconds.** Change settings, then tap
**Save profile**; they apply from the next session.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Child's name** | Shown in reports |
| **Maximum volume** | A ceiling on how loud AuraSOS can ever be (default 80%). It sits on top of your device's volume |
| **Maximum screen brightness** | A ceiling on screen glow (default 80%); the app also dims itself in the late calm-down stage |
| **High-frequency cut** | Removes high sounds above a chosen pitch (800–4000 Hz) for children sensitive to treble |
| **Water sounds** | Turns water-like textures off for children who dislike them |
| **Soften high tones** | Caps the sound at 1.2 kHz |
| **Animation** | What your child sees. See below |
| **Focus timer** | Length of the Focus timer, 5–60 minutes (default 20) |
| **SOS button starts** | *Gradual calm-down* or *Deep crisis anchor* |
| **Custom rules** | One per line. A rule such as `Block frequencies > 1800 Hz` is applied automatically |
| **Familiar voice** (phone apps) | See below |
| **Therapy report** | Copy a summary or CSV for your child's therapist |
| **About** | Version, offline status (web), and a short privacy summary |

### Choosing the animation
A grid of cards shows each option with a thumbnail. **Tap a card** to choose it; tap its **play button** to see it
full screen (tap the screen to try the touch effect, ✕ to leave). Nothing changes until you press **Save profile**.

| Animation | Looks like |
|---|---|
| **Automatic** | The best animation for each mode (default) |
| **Crystal polygons** | Three soft pastel 3D shapes turning slowly in space |
| **Polygon mandala** | Rings of polygons turning in opposite directions |
| **Starfield** | Soft stars drifting gently toward you |
| **Aurora** | Layers of teal and violet light over still water |
| **Light tunnel** | A glowing tunnel moving slowly forward |
| **Rising bubbles** | Translucent bubbles floating up |
| **Lanterns** | Warm lanterns on a pure black background (kind to light-sensitive eyes) |
| **Breathing orb** | A glowing orb that swells and softens like a slow breath (about 4 s in, 6 s out) |
| **Black screen** | Sound only, nothing lit |

If your child likes sound but finds the picture too much, use **Black screen**. If they love a picture but
sound is hard, set the volume low and keep the animation.

### Your voice, woven in (phone apps)
Record up to four short phrases (6 seconds each), such as *"You are safe. Take a deep breath."* Turn on
**Weave my voice into sessions** and choose how loud it is and how long to pause between phrases. During a session
your voice appears quietly, for a few seconds at a time, in rotation. Recordings never leave the device.
(The web version cannot record.)

## What helps: your child's history
Open the **graph icon** at the top right of the caregiver area.
- Summary tiles: sessions in total, in the last 7 days, and how often a session was rated "made it worse".
- **Most effective** appears once a mode has at least 3 ratings (so one lucky session isn't called "best").
- **By mode:** a bar of helped / no change / worse for each mode, with how many sessions it is based on.
- **When sessions happen:** morning, afternoon, evening, night.
- **Common triggers:** what you've tagged most often.
- **Recent sessions:** tap one to tag what happened before (loud noise, crowd, transition, change in routine,
  tired, hungry, waiting, screen taken away, unknown) and add a note.

## Reports for a therapist
In the caregiver area, **Copy report** puts a readable summary on the clipboard (profile, settings, how each mode did,
times of day, triggers, and a full session log). **Copy CSV** gives a spreadsheet-friendly version. The phone apps can also
**Save to files**. Reports are only shared if you share them.

## Privacy
Everything (profile, sessions, notes, recorded phrases) stays on your device. There are no accounts, ads or
analytics, and the app sends nothing anywhere. To delete everything, clear the app's data or uninstall it.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | Try |
|---|---|
| **No sound** (web app) | Browsers only allow sound after a touch. If you see **"Tap the screen to turn on sound"**, touch the screen once. Also check: device volume up and not muted, the **silent switch off** (iPhone), a Bluetooth speaker not connected to something else, and the tab not muted |
| **Sound is there but I can barely hear it** | The calming sounds are deliberately **low and soft** (much of it below 500 Hz), and the first 4 seconds fade in from silence. Small phone and tablet speakers reproduce low sounds poorly: use headphones or a speaker with some bass, and raise the device volume |
| **Sound stopped after a call or switching apps** (web) | Touch the screen once; it resumes. If the hint doesn't appear, reload the app |
| **Sound is too quiet** | The app's ceiling is 80% by default. Raise **Maximum volume**, and raise the device volume |
| **Can't unlock** | Hold *both* top corners together and keep holding for the full 3 seconds. Two fingers, both corners |
| **The picture looks stuttery** (web, older device) | Pick a lighter animation (Lanterns, Breathing orb or Light tunnel), or use the phone app |
| **A new version doesn't appear** (web) | Close every tab or the installed app and reopen it, or use **Settings → About → Reload app** |
| **"Not cached for offline use yet"** (web) | Open the app once over a secure (https) connection, wait a few seconds, then reload |
| **My settings reset** (web) | The browser cleared its site data (private windows and "clear data" do this). Saved settings live in the browser |
| **I can't record my voice** | Recording is available in the phone apps only, and needs microphone permission |
