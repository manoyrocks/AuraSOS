AuraSOS privacy

AuraSOS keeps everything on your device. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it sends nothing anywhere.

What the app stores

  • Your child's sensory profile: name or nickname, volume and brightness limits, chosen animation, sound preferences.
  • A log of sessions: when they ran, for how long, which mode, and the rating, triggers and notes you choose to add.
  • In the mobile apps only: voice phrases you record, saved as audio files on the device.

This data lives in your browser's storage (web) or the app's private storage (mobile). It is never uploaded.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-in, no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no third-party scripts, fonts or services.
  • After the first load the app runs fully offline. It makes no network requests to anyone but the site that served it.
  • The web version never asks for your microphone, camera or location.

Sharing reports

The therapy report is created on your device. Copying or saving it is your choice, and nothing is shared unless you share it.

Deleting your data

Clear this site's data in your browser settings, or uninstall the app. That removes the profile, session log and cached files.

Not medical advice

AuraSOS is a calming aid, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or replace guidance from a qualified professional.
